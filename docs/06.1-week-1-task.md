# Week 1 Detailed Task Distribution

## Week 1 Goal

Establish a stable enhancement baseline, inspect the completed single-vehicle implementation, remove the first architectural blockers and approve the contracts required for three team members to develop in parallel from Week 2 onward.

Week 1 is an enabling refactor, not a rewrite. The existing single-vehicle workflow must remain operational throughout the week.

## Working Rules

- Begin from the latest synchronized `main` branch and create separate feature branches for assigned work.
- Keep every change small, tested and reviewable through a pull request.
- Do not begin optional CANought or second-sensor work.
- Do not introduce multi-vehicle behaviour before the shared contracts are approved.
- Record commands, test results, screenshots, decisions and discovered risks as evidence.
- Raise contract conflicts immediately; do not create incompatible local assumptions.

## Week 1 Definition of Done

Week 1 is complete only when all of the following are true:

- The completed single-vehicle workflow still runs successfully.
- All pre-existing automated tests pass after the cleanup.
- Each touched component has appropriate regression or contract tests.
- `vehicleId` format and validation rules are approved.
- Event envelope and traceability fields are approved.
- Existing and perception signal paths are approved.
- Individual-twin and fleet-twin schemas are approved.
- REST API and diagnostic-action contracts are approved.
- Camera or LiDAR has been selected as the primary sensor.
- Velocitas and Kanto responsibility boundaries are documented.
- Critical hard-coded assumptions are removed or recorded as owned Week 2 issues.
- Each member can begin Week 2 independently without inventing a conflicting interface.

## End-of-Week Review

The team must conduct a final Week 1 review before beginning multi-vehicle implementation. Ankit records the decision as:

- **GO:** Baseline is stable, contracts are frozen and Week 2 work can proceed in parallel.
- **CONDITIONAL GO:** Only small, assigned contract corrections remain and do not block parallel work.
- **NO-GO:** Baseline tests fail, contracts conflict or component boundaries remain unclear. Resolve these issues before beginning Week 2 development.
